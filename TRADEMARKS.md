# Trademark policy

"Telinha Segura", the Espiadinha mark, the monogram, the lockups and the
app icons in [`brand/`](brand/) are trademarks of Coding For You Tecnologia Ltda.
("the marks"). The code is free software
([COPYRIGHT.md](COPYRIGHT.md)); the marks are not part of that licence.

## Why

The name promises something specific to families: every video was
screened by the Telinha Segura community, and the child's boundary is
enforced by our server. A fork that ships under the name without that
curation would break the promise in a way families cannot see. The policy
exists to protect children, not to restrict the code.

## What you may do without asking

- Say that your work is based on, or compatible with, Telinha Segura
  ("a fork of Telinha Segura", "works with Telinha Segura").
- Use the name in articles, reviews, talks and teaching about the project.
- Build, run and modify the code privately or for your organization's own
  use with the marks left in place, as long as you do not distribute it to
  the public under them.

## What needs written permission

- Publishing an app, a site or a service under the name "Telinha Segura",
  or a confusingly similar name, or with the marks. This covers any app
  store listing.
- Using the marks in a company, product or domain name.
- Selling merchandise with the marks.

## Forks

A public fork that ships to users must:

- replace the name, the marks and the app icons with its own;
- change the application identifiers (`tech.coding4u.*`) and the API
  hosts, so that it never presents itself as this service;
- state that it is not affiliated with or endorsed by Coding For You Tecnologia Ltda.
