In Telinha Segura, people choose the videos your child watches, not an algorithm. Parents, carers and people who work with children watch every video before it reaches your family's screen.

PEOPLE WATCHED IT FIRST
Each video is approved by at least two people on the review team, who answer a checklist on violence, language, consumerism, health and pace and say from what age it suits. Approved videos keep being reviewed. If something slips through, you report the video and it leaves your family's screen straight away.

A SCREEN THAT ENDS
No video rolling into the next. Videos sit on themed shelves, and the shelves end. When the day's time is up, the screen goes to sleep, with no countdown on screen.

YOU ARE IN CONTROL
• A profile for each child, with the right age band, such as 2 to 4 years
• A time limit and schedule for each one, and more time whenever you choose
• Only someone who knows the parents' PIN can leave child mode
• Your child's tablet linked to your family
• The rules are enforced on our server, not just on the screen

WHAT WE ASK FOR AND WHAT WE DON'T DO
For a child we ask only for a nickname, an avatar and an age band. We run no ads of our own, no in-app purchases and no usage analytics. Videos come from YouTube, and some may show YouTube's own ads. Telinha Segura is free.

WHO MAKES IT
Telinha Segura is made in Brazil and is available in Portuguese. See how the review team chooses videos at telinhasegura.coding4u.tech/curadoria and how we look after your data at telinhasegura.coding4u.tech/privacidade. Questions: telinhasegura.coding4u.tech/suporte.
